Material Knowledge

Know Your Construction Materials

Bricks

Bricks are commonly used for walls and masonry work. When ordering bricks, consider quality, grade, quantity and delivery requirements.

  • Confirm brick quality.
  • Calculate the required quantity.
  • Confirm transportation charges.
  • Check delivery location.

Cement

Cement is used in concrete, masonry, plastering and other construction work. Brand and quantity can affect the final project cost.

  • Confirm brand.
  • Check current market rate.
  • Calculate required bags.
  • Ask about bulk supply.

Sand

Construction sand can be required for concrete, plastering and masonry. Always confirm the appropriate type for your project.

  • Confirm sand type.
  • Confirm quantity.
  • Confirm delivery charges.
  • Confirm intended application.

Stone Crush

Stone crush or bajri is commonly used in concrete and other construction work. Different sizes may be required for different applications.

  • 3/4 inch crush
  • 1/2 inch crush
  • Confirm required size.
  • Confirm trolley quantity.
